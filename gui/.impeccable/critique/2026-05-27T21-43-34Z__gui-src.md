## What's Working

1. **JetBrains Mono commit is high-leverage and low-friction.** Single `--font-mono` token swap propagates to 40+ existing consumers without per-surface edits.
2. **Chrome-surface rule is the right unification.** Reference dialogs now share the top-toolbar tone — "the same app, with a panel raised" not "a modal from elsewhere."
3. **Validator + bottom-chrome handles get the at-rest hairline.** Discoverability gain at a one-pixel visual cost.
