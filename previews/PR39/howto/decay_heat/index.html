Add decay heat to a transient

Decay heat reaches a model through the kinetics: a DecayHeatSource turns a decay heat contribution into a power, and PointKinetics adds it to the fission power. The fuel, heated by the kinetics' total power P, then sees both. The physics is in Decay heat.

Point STREAM at the standards

The fission product tables are published standards that STREAM does not distribute. Tell it where they are, once per session:

DecayHeat.standards_dir!("/path/to/standards")

or set the environment variable STREAM_DECAY_HEAT_STANDARDS before starting Julia. Then read a standard and source:

fp = DecayHeat.FissionProducts(DecayHeat.ANS14, DecayHeat.U235)          # ANSI/ANS-5.1-2014fp_gamma = DecayHeat.FissionProducts(DecayHeat.JAERI91, DecayHeat.U235_gamma)  # gamma only

The standards are ANS73, ANS14 and JAERI91, and the sources U235, U238, and, for JAERI, their _beta and _gamma parts. DecayHeat.read_standard returns the raw decay constants and strengths.

Build the contribution

Contributions add and scale, so a component's total is written as a sum. For fuel, the fission products and the U-238 capture chain:

heat = fp + DecayHeat.U238CaptureChain(0.5)   # 0.5 captures in U-238 per fission

The rest of this page uses a made-up fission product fit, so that it runs without the tables:

using STREAMusing STREAM.Components: PointKinetics, ReactivityController, StateMachineusing ModelingToolkit: @namedfp = DecayHeat.FissionProducts([1.0, 0.05, 1e-3, 1e-5], [3.0, 0.15, 3e-3, 3e-5])heat = fp + DecayHeat.U238CaptureChain(0.5)
DecayHeat.Sum of 2 contributions:
  FissionProducts(4 groups)
  U238CaptureChain(0.5)

The total is a sum of its parts, and is called like any of them. At shutdown it is, as a share of the operating power for 200 MeV per fission:

heat(0.0) / 200
0.062162499999999996

Do not add DecayHeat.Fissions to a model whose power comes from PointKinetics: after a scram the kinetics already compute the fission power, delayed neutrons included.

Turn it into a power, with a trip clock

DecayHeatSource needs the operating power P0, in the units the kinetics use, and the machine that decides when the reactor shuts down. Until the machine enters :SCRAM, the source holds its value at shutdown, the decay heat of a reactor at power. After, it decays.

machine = StateMachine(; initial_state=:SCRAM, initial_time=10.0)   # a scram at t = 10 ssource = DecayHeat.DecayHeatSource(heat, machine; P0=1.0)
DecayHeatSource at fission rate P0/Q = 0.005, saturated, shut down in SCRAM of StateMachine(SCRAM since t = 10.0 s)

    FissionProducts(4 groups)
    U238CaptureChain(0.5)
[round(source(t); sigdigits=4) for t in (0.0, 10.0, 70.0, 610.0)]

The source is flat until the scram and decays after. Here the machine is built already tripped, which is a scram at a known time. In a model where the trip comes from a transition, pass the machine the protection system uses, or the ReactivityController holding it.

Give it to the kinetics

ctrl = ReactivityController((state, t_state, t) -> state === :SCRAM ? -0.06 : 0.0;                            machine=StateMachine())source = DecayHeat.DecayHeatSource(heat, ctrl; P0=1.0)@named pk = PointKinetics(ctrl; power_input=source)

The kinetics start at total power 1, with fission making up 1 - source(0) of it. Heat the fuel with pk.P, the total:

connections = [..., fuel.power ~ pk.P * P_rated]

Units. P0 sets the units of the source, which must be those of the kinetics. A model whose kinetics run in units of the rated power, as above, takes P0 = 1. One running in watts takes the rated power in watts.

Finite irradiation

The source assumes the reactor ran long enough to saturate every group, T = Inf. After a short run less has built up. Pass the operating time in seconds:

short = DecayHeat.DecayHeatSource(heat, machine; P0=1.0, T=3600.0)(saturated=DecayHeat.DecayHeatSource(heat, machine; P0=1.0)(610.0), one_hour=short(610.0))
(saturated = 0.025011220813409483, one_hour = 0.009252808377546842)

Turn a decay heat contribution into the power_input a PointKinetics takes.

FissionProducts(λ, α) <: AbstractDecayHeat FissionProducts(standard, source; dir=standards_dir()) <: AbstractDecayHeat

Decay of fission products, the largest decay heat contribution, as the summed exponential fit the standards publish,

U238CaptureChain(R) <: AbstractDecayHeat

Decay heat from the U239 and Np239 that neutron capture in U238 leaves behind,

standards_dir!(path) -> String

Point read_standard at the directory holding the decay heat tables.
