Tutorials

The tutorials teach STREAM.jl by building models, one step at a time, from a single loop to a loss of flow in a pool reactor. Each builds its model by hand, so you see every component and connection, and each ends by checking the result against physics you can work out on paper. Read them in order: each assumes what the ones before it taught.

TutorialWhat you learn
1A first loopcomponents, connections, compiling, a steady solve, reading results
2Hydraulic networksparallel branches, junctions, gravity and buoyancy-driven flow
3Pump coastdowninertia, inputs that change in time, transients
4A fuel plate between two channelssolid conduction, thermal coupling, axial power shapes, safety margins
5Reactivity insertion with feedbackpoint kinetics, temperature feedback, the prompt jump
6Loss of flow in a pool reactoreverything together: trips, a check valve, decay heat, flow reversal, margins over time

Every tutorial can be downloaded as a Julia script or a Jupyter notebook from the link at its top, and run as it is.

Once you know your way around, the How-to guides answer specific questions, and the Explanation pages give the physics behind the models.
