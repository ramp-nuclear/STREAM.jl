## Tech Debt

### Phase 9 — `_channel_base_eqs` dead parameter (non-blocking)
`_channel_base_eqs` accepts `t_inlet` keyword argument (components.jl:207) but never uses it inside the helper. Both `ChannelAndContacts` and `ChannelHeatFlux` pass it and compute `T_inlet` independently in their own loops. No correctness impact. **Recommendation:** Remove `t_inlet` from helper signature in v0.3 cleanup.

### Phase 9 — THERM-03 proxy validation (non-blocking)
THERM-03 was validated via ChannelHeatFlux (scalar `T_wall_p`) rather than ChannelAndContacts (pinned ThermalPorts) directly. Algebraically equivalent under uniform T_wall. Intentional design decision documented in 09-02-PLAN.md. **Recommendation:** Add direct ChannelAndContacts test in v0.3 for completeness.
