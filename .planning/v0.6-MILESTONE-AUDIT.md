### Known Architectural Limitation (not a broken wire)

**Callable Pump + Flapper(SymbolicContinuousCallback) incompatible:** `Pump(dP_fn)` (PUMP-01) and `Flapper` with embedded MTK continuous event cannot coexist in the same ODEProblem. LOF scenario uses `Pump(0.0) + Inertia` + `Flapper(use_callback=false)` + external `ContinuousCallback` as workaround. Documented in `flapper.jl` docstring, `24.1-01-SUMMARY.md`. PUMP-01 independently validated in `test_pump.jl` PUMP-03. Deferred to v0.7+.
