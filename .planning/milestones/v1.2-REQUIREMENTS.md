## Note on requirements format

v1.2's "requirements" are the 14 phase goals from the milestone ROADMAP plus the design contract in `PRODUCT.md` / `DESIGN.md`. There is no atomic `XYZ-NN` requirement list because v1.2 was an experience milestone (GUI redesign) rather than a physics milestone — the granular criteria live inside each phase's PLAN.md and SUMMARY.md, and the cross-cutting design criteria live in `PRODUCT.md` / `DESIGN.md`.

For traceability, the historical v1.1 atomic-requirement format is preserved at `.planning/archive/v1.1/REQUIREMENTS.md`.
