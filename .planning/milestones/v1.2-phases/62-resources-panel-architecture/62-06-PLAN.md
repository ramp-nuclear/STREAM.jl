<integration_seam_for_popover_creation>
The `+ button` on each group header opens the same `+ New…` popover used by the field-level ResourceReferencePicker (62-08). Two strategies:
1. **Centralized popover host:** A single `<ResourceCreationPopover>` component mounted at App.tsx level, controlled by a store action `openResourceCreationPopover(kind, anchorEl)`. Both 62-06 `+ button` and 62-08 ReferencePicker `+ New…` dispatch the action.
2. **Local popover instance per consumer:** 62-06 mounts its own popover, 62-08 mounts its own. They share the inner ResourceEditor component but not the Popover wrapper.

Phase 62 picks **strategy 2** for simplicity (local popover per consumer). The shared piece is the editor form (62-08 implements `<GeometryResourceEditor>` and `<PowerShapeResourceEditor>` once; both call sites consume them). 62-06's `+ button` opens a local Popover anchored to itself, with the same editor inside. Until 62-08 ships those editors, this plan stubs the `+ button` to `console.log("create resource of kind=" + kind)` AND a TODO comment marking the integration point.
</integration_seam_for_popover_creation>
